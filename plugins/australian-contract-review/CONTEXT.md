# Contract review vocabulary

**Review playbook**: versioned preferred positions, fallbacks, risk appetite and
approval thresholds. It supplies commercial instructions, never legal authority.

**Risk rating**: evidenced legal, commercial or operational exposure. A red rating
is an escalation, not a declaration of invalidity; green never authorises signing.

**Readiness**: completeness of a review deliverable for a qualified lawyer.
It is separate from traffic-light risk and approval.

**Notice candidate**: a date calculated from evidenced terms and anchors, pending
human confirmation of interpretation and service mechanics.

**Review label**: `DRAFT - LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED` on every output.
