# State liability verification route

Snapshot: 2026-10-13. Each publisher was attempted for this revision; VERIFIED
below describes the inspected Act/Part location or expressly stated text, not
approval of a clause or a nationwide contracting-out conclusion. A qualified
lawyer must recheck point-in-time text, amendments and claim-specific application
at the official publisher. Contractual allocation, apportionment and insurance
response require separate analysis.

Queensland's dated HTML and current PDF download exposed different version dates
(HTML URL: 22 July 2026; PDF: 1 July 2024). Both inspected texts contain the same
s 7(3) wording and Chapter 2 Part 2 location. Resolve version/application currency
at use rather than presenting the PDF's date as proof of full current coverage.
SA and NT Act/Part observations are similarly limited to the inspected versions.

For each jurisdiction record the claim, scope/exclusions, concurrent wrongdoers,
contribution/indemnity interaction, transition, exact contracting-out text and
version. Where express allocation is permitted, check formalities and operative
wording, then assess remaining mandatory-law and insurance constraints. Silence
in a Part is not evidence either of permission or of prohibition. An inaccessible
publisher requires verify against the named source and stops the affected conclusion.

## Commonwealth apportionment leads

Use `$check-commonwealth-legislation` on legislation.gov.au:

- VERIFIED location: [ASIC Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00819/latest/text),
  Part 2 Division 2 Subdivision GA, ss 12GP–12GW, compilation C2026C00429,
  19 September 2026. Section 12GP addresses damages claims under s 12GF for
  economic loss/property damage caused by a s 12DA contravention. Verify exact
  coverage and any contracting-out conclusion separately.
- [Competition and Consumer Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00109/latest/text),
  Part VIA, s 87CB onwards — verify against legislation.gov.au; this is a lead,
  not an assertion of current scope or contracting-out effect.
- [Corporations Act 2001](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00818/latest/text),
  Part 7.10 Division 2A, s 1041L onwards — verify against legislation.gov.au;
  this is a lead, not an assertion of current scope or contracting-out effect.

A state choice-of-law or exclusion clause does not establish that it displaces
one of these regimes. Case-law interpretation needs separate official-judgment
verification and lawyer review; the table does not certify clause enforceability.
