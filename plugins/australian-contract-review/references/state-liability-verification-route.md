# State liability verification route

Snapshot: 2026-10-13; recheck point-in-time text and application for the actual
claim and governing jurisdiction. A qualified lawyer must verify every state
citation at the official publisher named below. Contractual risk allocation,
statutory apportionment and insurance response are distinct questions.

For each row locate the applicable proportionate-liability provisions, scope,
exclusions, contracting-out rule, indemnity/contribution interaction and transition.
Do not assume every jurisdiction uses an Act titled Civil Liability Act, or that
an express exclusion clause is effective throughout Australia.

Verified scoped example: [Civil Liability Act 2003 (Qld)](https://www.legislation.qld.gov.au/view/pdf/current/act-2003-016),
official authorised text current as at 1 July 2024, s 7(3), excludes Chapter 2
Part 2 and Chapter 3 from its express-contract preservation provision. Chapter 2
Part 2 contains proportionate liability. Do not read that provision as general
permission to contract out of proportionate liability. Verify claim scope and
current amendments before concluding the effect of a particular indemnity.

NSW lead: Civil Liability Act 2002, s 3A and Part 4 — verify against
legislation.nsw.gov.au. The publisher returned 403 during this snapshot's attempted
text/XML/PDF checks; contracting-out permission and exact section effects are
NOT VERIFIED here and must not be asserted from memory. Other jurisdictions'
section numbers and contracting-out permissions are not established by this
snapshot: verify against their official publishers. If permission is confirmed,
record exact statutory words, version and applicable claim before proposing an
express exclusion; assess consequences for contribution, indemnities and insurance.

Also check any relevant Commonwealth apportionment regime using
`$check-commonwealth-legislation` on legislation.gov.au; a state choice-of-law
clause is not evidence that it displaces a Commonwealth rule. Case-law questions
need separate official-judgment verification and lawyer interpretation.
