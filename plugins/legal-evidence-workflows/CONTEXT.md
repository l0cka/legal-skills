# Evidence workflows

Vocabulary for the `legal-evidence-workflows` plugin.

## Language

**Source ID**:
The stable identifier assigned to one supplied document or coherent file
group by the document index or the source register. Once assigned it never
changes; every other workflow cites it.
_Avoid_: exhibit number (unless supplied by the practitioner), doc #

**Pinpoint**:
The page, paragraph, section, email timestamp or other locator inside a
source that supports a row. A row without a usable pinpoint says so; a
pinpoint is never invented.

**Evidence character**:
The provenance label given to a statement: `contemporaneous record`,
`reported recollection`, `allegation`, `inference` or `unclear`. It says
where the statement comes from, never whether it is true, admissible or
weighty.
_Avoid_: reliability, strength

**Conflict ID / divergence ID**:
The link between two or more entries that give competing accounts of the
same fact. Entries stay separate; the ID is the only thing that joins them.

**Divergence class**:
`date or time`, `amount or quantity`, `sequence`, `participant`, `wording
or characterisation`, `omission` or `internal` — what kind of difference the
inconsistency map found. A divergence may carry more than one class.

**Candidate basis**:
The privilege ground a document's face or the practitioner's instruction
suggests (legal advice, litigation, without prejudice, confidential).
Always labelled `candidate`; the practitioner decides the claim.
_Avoid_: privileged, protected

**Waiver flag**:
A recorded fact — circulation outside the client and its advisers,
forwarding, quotation, reference in a non-privileged document — that a
practitioner would weigh on waiver. A flag is never a finding that privilege
is lost.

**Review record**:
The state of human review of a deliverable: `unreviewed`, `corrected` or
`verified`, with reviewer details only when supplied.

**Supplied-source boundary**:
The statement in every deliverable that it covers only the supplied and
readable documents and claims nothing beyond them.
