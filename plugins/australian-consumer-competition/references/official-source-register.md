## Guidance and proposal leads

- [ACCC environmental claims guide](https://www.accc.gov.au/about-us/publications/a-guide-to-making-environmental-claims-for-business), published 12 December 2023: guidance on substantiation and presentation; do not use old guidance penalty figures as current law.
- [ACCC price displays](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/pricing/price-displays): guidance on purchase journeys and misleading drip pricing.
- [ACCC notification waivers](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/mergers-and-acquisitions/notification-waivers) and [threshold guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/mergers-and-acquisitions/thresholds-for-notifying-acquisitions): guidance subordinate to current Act/instrument text.
- [ACCC Product Safety](https://www.productsafety.gov.au/): discover product-specific standards, bans, recalls and reporting guidance; verify each legal instrument at its official publisher.
