# Merger pathway checklist

Verify against the [Notification of Acquisitions Determination 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L00753/latest),
F2026C00002 (1 January 2026), and the [current CCA](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00109/latest/text)
at the contract and completion dates. Snapshot 2026-10-13; a qualified lawyer decides.

These screening figures are not a complete notification decision:

| Route | Instrument hooks and screening figures |
| --- | --- |
| Business/share acquisitions | ss 1-9, 1-10, 1-12, 2-1: combined Australian revenue ≥$200m AND (acquired revenue ≥$50m OR transaction value ≥$250m) |
| Very large acquirer | ss 1-10, 1-13, 2-2: acquirer group Australian revenue ≥$500m and acquired revenue ≥$10m |
| Serial acquisitions | ss 1-11, 1-14, 2-3: examine 3-year competitive/substitutable acquisitions, accumulated revenue ≥$50m/≥$10m respectively, with the small-acquisition test (<$2m) and other exclusions; do not apply a geographic shortcut |
| Standalone assets | ss 1-12, 2-4: combined revenue ≥$200m with value ≥$200m, or acquirer revenue ≥$500m with value ≥$50m; verify asset scope |

Also verify the current [Notification of Acquisitions—Forms Determination 2025](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L01604/latest) for notification and public-benefit forms; current form text was unavailable in this snapshot and remains not verified.

Check Australian connection, connected entities, consolidated revenue without double
counting, financial year, contract-date measures, value methodology, multi-party rules,
small acquisition exclusions, indexation and currency conversion. Record calculations
with financial evidence; a missing value is not zero.

Check Part 2 Division 2 exceptions and Part 3 targeted supermarket and minority-share
classes with their separate application dates under Part 10. Verify current CCA
control/associate provisions and exemptions; minority ownership alone does not establish
an exemption. Recheck amendments included in compilation 167 (16 September 2026), rather than
using only the 2024 Act; trace any later amendments at legislation.gov.au.

Waiver checklist: CCA ss 51ABU–51ABV; instrument ss 6-3–6-6 and 7-50. Verify form,
information, fee, publication/confidentiality and any special process. General case
s 6-4 requires refusal after the specified 25-business-day period if no determination
has been made; silence is not approval. A waiver application is not a waiver grant.
Retain the written determination and reasons. A granted waiver removes notification,
not substantive s 50 review. Check current stay, conditions, review and staleness before
any completion recommendation. This workflow never lodges or permits closing.
