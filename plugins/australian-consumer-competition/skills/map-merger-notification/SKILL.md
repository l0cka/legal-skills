# Map Merger Notification

Australian Commonwealth law and ACL application in the identified state or territory.
Snapshot 2026-10-13; verify law at the conduct date and intended decision horizon.
Record assumptions explicitly; no missing fact defaults to a favourable answer.
Every output requires review by a qualified lawyer and does not replace professional judgment.

Read the [source and control method](../../references/consumer-competition-source-and-control-method.md)
and [official source register](../../references/official-source-register.md).
Verify every statutory citation at legislation.gov.au through `$check-commonwealth-legislation`;
for state or territory application use its official legislation publisher. Record provision,
version, effective date, checked date, observation and evidence state. If unavailable, say
'verify against <official source>' and leave the affected proposition not verified.

## Workflow

1. Fix legal as-at, contract and completion dates; obtain parties/connected entities,
   shares/assets, control/associate rights, Australian nexus, financial years, Australian
   revenue, global transaction value, land/supermarket facts and acquisition history.
2. Read the [merger checklist](../../references/merger-pathways.md). Verify the 2024
   reform Act commencement, current CCA Part IVA and ss 45AW/45AY/51ABE/189, and
   latest thresholds and Notification of Acquisitions—Forms determinations at legislation.gov.au. The mandatory and suspensory framework
   began 1 January 2026; check transitional decisions and later amendments separately.
3. Build independent threshold rows for business/share, very-large-group, serial and
   standalone-asset acquisitions. Use current definitions, contract-date financial evidence,
   connected entities, no double counting, accumulation/exclusions, indexation and currency.
   Show arithmetic and missing inputs; a single target-turnover figure is insufficient.
4. Verify Part 2 exceptions, Part 3 targeted classes and Part 10 application dates alongside
   current control/minority-interest rules. Treat an exemption as a reasoned, sourced branch,
   never an inference from a small holding, group label or apparently low overlap.
5. Map notification and waiver alternatives using CCA ss 51ABU–51ABV and determination
   ss 6-3–6-6/7-50: form, information, fee, timing, publication/confidentiality and suitability.
   Verify ACCC guidance as guidance. Silence or an application is not a grant; waiver does
   not resolve substantive s 50 risk. Leave actual applications and payment to authorised humans.
6. Identify stay/standstill, review, conditions, material changes and staleness issues and
   evidence needed before completion. Raise gun-jumping/integration concerns to the lawyer.
   For competitor information exchange use `$assess-cartel-and-rpm-risk`.
7. Return pathway alternatives, missing facts, source evidence and a human closing gate.
   Never say the acquisition may close based solely on this workflow.

## Result contract

Lead with `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW`, `READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS`, `NOT READY`
or `OUTSIDE SCOPE`. Evidence states apply to individual propositions separately.

Transaction perimeter; threshold calculation matrix; exemptions and targeted classes; waiver/notification evidence and dependencies; standstill risks; unresolved facts and qualified lawyer decision gate.
Every finding records source, version, pinpoint, checked date, assumptions, evidence
state, missing step and responsible qualified lawyer. No output is approval to act.

## Fail closed

Missing group revenue, historical acquisitions, control evidence or instrument currency blocks a definitive notifiable/not-notifiable conclusion. No deemed approval, filing or closing permission.
