# Unfair contract terms checklist

## Verify coverage before conclusions

Verify the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010* (Cth), Schedule 2 (Australian
Consumer Law), ss 23–28 and 224 against the current or point-in-time text on
[legislation.gov.au](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00109/latest/text).
Checked snapshot: C2026C00405, 16 September 2026, inspected on 2026-10-13.
This checklist paraphrases the statutory leads; it is not legal authority.
A qualified lawyer must confirm application and any unfairness conclusion.

Record the making, renewal and variation dates and verify the transition in
[the 2022 amending Act](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2022A00054/asmade/text),
Schedule 2 and its application provisions. The penalty regime commenced on
9 November 2023, covering relevant new/renewed contracts and varied/added terms.
Do not treat every older term as newly subject to penalties merely because
one term changed. Check the rule applying standard-form changes to the contract.

For ACL s 23 coverage, distinguish personal/household consumer contracts from
small business contracts. For the latter, test the supply/land criterion and
whether at least one party meets either limb: fewer than 100 employees or
turnover below $10 million for the specified preceding income year. Record
employee counting (regular/systematic casuals and part-time full-time equivalents)
and statutory turnover adjustments under s 23(5)–(7). There is no post-reform
upfront-price ceiling for this ACL small-business test. Do not substitute the
Privacy Act small-business threshold or assume B2B is exempt.

Assess standard form under s 27: bargaining power, repeat use, pre-preparation,
real opportunity to negotiate and particular transaction characteristics.
Minor edits, selecting preset options or negotiating someone else's contract
do not necessarily remove standard-form status. Verify exclusions under ss 26,
28 and 28A; financial products/services need their separate statutory regime.

## Assess the term and whole contract

Under ACL s 24 examine all three elements: significant imbalance, absence of
reasonable necessity to protect legitimate interests, and detriment if used.
Record the advantaged party's evidence of necessity, transparency and the
contract as a whole. Clear wording alone does not cure substantive unfairness.
The grey list in s 25 is illustrative, not an automatic invalidity rule.

## Grey-list map: ACL s 25

For every row record clause/location, context, legitimate-interest evidence,
detriment, proposed mitigation and lawyer decision. Verify each paragraph
against the official source above; never rely on the model's memory.

| Paragraph | Risk to inspect | Drafting response to consider |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) | One party can evade or restrict performance | Define narrow triggers and proportionate remedies |
| (b) | One-sided termination | Objective grounds, cure and reciprocal remedies where appropriate |
| (c) | Asymmetric breach or exit penalties | Relate charges to justified loss; avoid punitive forfeiture |
| (d) | Unilateral changes to terms | Defined reasons, advance notice and effective cancellation |
| (e) | One-sided renewal control | Prominent renewal terms, reminder and workable opt-out |
| (f) | Price changes without an exit right | Notify before effect and allow penalty-free cancellation |
| (g) | Unilateral changes to product/service characteristics | Define permitted scope; allow exit for material adverse change |
| (h) | One party decides meaning or breach conclusively | Objective tests and dispute access |
| (i) | Limits on responsibility for agents | Align responsibility with control and applicable law |
| (j) | Detrimental assignment without consent | Consent or protection against material detriment |
| (k) | Restrictions on suing | Preserve statutory remedies and access to courts |
| (l) | Limits on evidence in proceedings | Remove procedural disadvantages |
| (m) | Burdens of proof shifted to one party | Avoid unsupported evidential presumptions |
| (n) | Further prescribed classes | Check applicable regulations at use |

## Practical sweep and issues table

Inspect auto-renewal, unilateral variation, all-sales-final wording, broad
suspension, acceleration of future fees, unlimited customer indemnities,
low/zero supplier caps, discretionary refunds, conclusive certificates and
exclusive distant forums. A generic savings clause does not repair conflicting
operative wording. Do not call a term fair merely because this bank supplied it.

Return: issue ID; priority; clause; coverage evidence; s 24 analysis and s 25
lead; consumer impact; source/version/date/evidence state; proposed rewrite or
options; missing facts; reviewer decision. Use `potential UCT risk`, not a
conclusive declaration of invalidity. Preserve non-excludable consumer rights
independently of the UCT review.
