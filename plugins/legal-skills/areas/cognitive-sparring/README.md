# Legal Cognitive Sparring

This practice area spars with a qualified lawyer. It does not produce final legal
advice, a filing, or a message to anyone else. Jurisdiction is lawyer-supplied.
Its skills state no legal rule of any legal system.

The `lawCheckedOn` date records a review of that boundary. There is no statute
or case in this area to re-check. The next review confirms the skills still
refuse a default legal system and still refuse fabricated citations.

## Skills

Core sparring:

- `pressure-test-argument` critiques premises, leaps, missing authorities,
  adverse facts, and weaker framings. It does not rewrite the critique as
  final advice.
- `test-understanding` asks short questions, demands sources, and marks gaps.
  It does not lecture or invent an authority.
- `oppose-as-counsel` puts the strongest counters the lawyer must answer.
  Speculation is labelled. It does not advise the opposing party.
- `steelman-then-attack` restates the position at its strongest, then attacks
  that steelman. The two phases stay separate.
- `role-play-decision-maker` answers as the skeptical judge, tribunal member,
  regulator, or other decision-maker the lawyer names. It does not predict an
  outcome as a certainty.

Structure and gaps:

- `map-assumptions` lists explicit and hidden assumptions, ranks the ones the
  case turns on, and proposes a way to falsify each.
- `find-blind-spots` lists questions, facts, parties, procedures, and
  authorities not yet addressed, split into must-check and nice-to-have.
- `clarify-burden-and-standard` states who bears which burden, under what
  standard, and what evidence would meet or fail it on the supplied facts.
- `red-team-evidence` challenges provenance, the hearsay or weight risks the
  lawyer has framed, and the attacks a competent opponent would make. It does
  not decide admissibility.

Strategy:

- `pressure-test-strategy` challenges forum, timing, settlement, and
  escalation choices for unstated risks and path dependence. It does not file
  or contact a counterparty.
- `simplify-without-losing-precision` restates the position in language a
  non-lawyer client could follow, then flags where that restatement hid a
  material nuance.
- `drill-oral-advocacy` runs a short hostile question drill or an opening or
  closing drill, and scores clarity, concessions, and recovery. It does not
  invent transcript authority.

## Boundary

Every skill reads `references/sparring-contract.md` first. The lawyer supplies
jurisdiction, forum, governing law, facts, and authorities. Where the record
is silent, the skill says it cannot tell from the supplied record. A qualified
lawyer owns the outcome.

## Permissions

No MCP server, app, hook, or write action is bundled. The lawyer chooses what
to paste into the session. The workflows do not file, send, or publish
anything.
