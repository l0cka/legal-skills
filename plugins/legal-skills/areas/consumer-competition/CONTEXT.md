# Consumer and competition vocabulary

**Profile**: a controlled factual map of the business, markets, products and owners;
never proof of statutory coverage, legal compliance or an approved course of action.

**Claim inventory**: the exact words, visuals, audience, channel, date and evidence
for each representation, including the complete purchase journey.

**Notification map**: separate evidence-backed merger threshold, exemption, waiver
and standstill pathways; it does not permit completion.

**Safety clock**: the evidenced awareness or recall-action event and candidate
reporting period, escalated immediately to the responsible lawyer.

**Watch item**: a dated source and status to recheck on demand; never a live monitor.
