# Commercial drafting vocabulary

**Drafting profile**: a versioned map of the drafting party, style and approved
standard positions. It cannot establish legal application or supply matter facts.

**Instruction table**: each transaction fact or choice with its source, date,
confirmer and evidence state. Missing values remain unknown.

**Original clause bank**: optional drafting examples authored for this area.
They are not approved firm precedents or verified statements of legal effect.

**Review marker**: `[REVIEW REQUIRED – <issue ID: missing fact or decision>]`
at each affected location; it must match an entry in the issues register.

**First draft**: a complete document structure with supported operative wording
and visible unresolved choices, labelled `DRAFT – LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED`.
It is never approved, executable advice or ready to sign or publish.
