## Provision leads in the model WHS Act

All `NOT VERIFIED` at the snapshot. Section numbers vary between
jurisdictions; check the local Act.

- Primary duty of care of a PCBU: s 19, read with s 17 (management of risks)
  and s 18 (what is reasonably practicable).
- Duties of officers: s 27, with the due diligence elements in s 27(5):
  knowledge of WHS matters; understanding of operations, hazards and risks;
  appropriate resources and processes; processes for receiving and responding
  to incident, hazard and risk information; processes for complying with
  duties; and verification of those resources and processes.
- Duties of workers and other persons: ss 28 and 29.
- Offences: s 31 (Category 1), s 32 (Category 2), s 33 (Category 3) and, in
  jurisdictions that have enacted one, an industrial manslaughter offence.
- Notifiable incidents: s 35 (meaning), s 36 (serious injury or illness),
  s 37 (dangerous incident), s 38 (duty to notify), s 39 (duty to preserve
  incident sites). The December 2025 model amendments change these sections
  and add s 39A; apply them only where the jurisdiction has enacted them.
- Consultation: ss 46 to 49 (duty to consult workers) and Part 5.
- Psychosocial risks: the model WHS Regulations provisions on psychosocial
  hazards (regulation 55A onwards in the model), with the Managing
  psychosocial hazards at work code of practice where approved.
- Prosecution time limit: s 232. Identify only; route any candidate date to
  `$compute-procedural-deadlines`.

## Victorian provision leads

All `NOT VERIFIED` at the snapshot. Victoria's Act uses different concepts
(employer, self-employed person, officer liability for a body corporate's
offence) and different section numbers. Never map a model WHS Act section onto
Victoria.

- Employer duties to employees: s 21; to others: s 23.
- Officer liability: s 144 (liability of officers of a body corporate), not a
  positive due diligence duty in the model form.
- Notification and site preservation: ss 38 and 39.
- Workplace manslaughter: s 39G.
- Consultation: ss 35 and 36.
- Psychological health: the Occupational Health and Safety (Psychological
  Health) Regulations 2025 and the supporting compliance code.
