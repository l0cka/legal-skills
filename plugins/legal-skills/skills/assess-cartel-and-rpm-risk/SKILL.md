# Assess Cartel and RPM Risk

Australian Commonwealth competition law under CCA Part IV, with Part VII immunity
and Part VIII RPM provisions. Confirm entity, territorial reach and any sectoral overlay.
Snapshot 2026-10-13; verify law at the conduct date and intended decision horizon.
Record assumptions explicitly; no missing fact defaults to a favourable answer.
Every output requires review by a qualified lawyer and does not replace professional judgment.

Read the [source and control method](../../references/consumer-competition-source-and-control-method.md)
and [official source register](../../references/official-source-register.md).
Verify every statutory citation at legislation.gov.au through `$check-commonwealth-legislation`;
for state or territory application use its official legislation publisher. Record provision,
version, effective date, checked date, observation and evidence state. If unavailable, say
'verify against <official source>' and leave the affected proposition not verified.

## Workflow

1. Capture exact conduct, parties, competitor relationships, communications, agreement
   history, products, market hypotheses, dates and proposed next steps. Preserve evidence;
   do not contact counterparties or suggest concealment or destruction.
2. Verify CCA Part IV Division 1 at legislation.gov.au, especially s 45AD and making/giving
   effect provisions. Test price fixing, output restrictions, market allocation and bid rigging,
   the competition condition and exceptions in ss 45AL–45AU. Verify joint-venture
   exceptions (ss 45AO–45AP) and anti-overlap provisions (including ss 45AQ–45AR)
   from their full text; a collaboration label proves nothing.
   Add a separate s 45 row for making/giving effect to anti-competitive agreements
   and concerted practices, including competitor information exchange that falls
   short of a cartel provision. Verify the purpose/effect/likely-effect substantial
   lessening of competition test and applicable exceptions; no cartel finding does
   not mean no competition risk.
3. Analyse RPM separately under CCA s 48 and Part VIII (ss 96–100), not ACL s 48 pricing.
   Check minimum resale-price pressure, discount restrictions, supply threats and whether a
   recommendation is enforced. Do not require proof of market power before raising RPM risk.
4. Separately verify s 47 exclusive dealing and its competition test, and s 46 substantial
   market power and purpose/effect/likely-effect test. Define market and evidence gaps;
   a harsh contract or low market share alone does not decide legality.
5. Verify authorisation under ss 88/90 and relevant notification under s 93.
   Separately verify collective bargaining notification (Part VII Division 2
   Subdivision B, ss 93AB–93AG) and class exemptions (Part VII Division 3,
   ss 95AA–95AC), including the actual instrument, eligible parties/conduct,
   notices, effective period, exclusions and objection/withdrawal provisions.
   If that text or instrument is unavailable, write verify against legislation.gov.au
   and leave protection unverified; do not assert a class exemption applies. Identify scope, conduct, applicants, commencement,
   conditions, expiry/revocation and evidence of an effective instrument. Do not treat
   notification as generic cartel immunity or an application as protection already in force.
6. Give urgent lawyer escalation for possible cartel/criminal exposure, proposed risky conduct
   and immunity uncertainty. Use `$route-case-citation` before relying on cases; enforcement
   allegations are not findings. Provide lawful review alternatives without facilitating evasion.

## Result contract

Lead with `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW`, `READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS`, `NOT READY`
or `OUTSIDE SCOPE`. Evidence states apply to individual propositions separately.

Conduct-by-provision matrix; market/competitor assumptions; cartel/s 45 agreements and concerted practices/RPM/exclusive-dealing/market-power distinctions; immunity scope and evidence; escalation priorities; qualified lawyer decisions.
Every finding records source, version, pinpoint, checked date, assumptions, evidence
state, missing step and responsible qualified lawyer. No output is approval to act.

## Fail closed

No lawful conclusion from an absent complaint, pending authorisation or unverified notification. Missing conduct or market evidence qualifies or blocks the affected test; never conclude criminal liability or provide collusion tactics.
