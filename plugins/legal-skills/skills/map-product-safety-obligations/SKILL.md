# Map Product Safety Obligations

Australian Commonwealth law and ACL application in the identified state or territory.
Snapshot 2026-10-13; verify law at the conduct date and intended decision horizon.
Record assumptions explicitly; no missing fact defaults to a favourable answer.
Every output requires review by a qualified lawyer and does not replace professional judgment.

Read the [source and control method](../../references/consumer-competition-source-and-control-method.md)
and [official source register](../../references/official-source-register.md).
Verify every statutory citation at legislation.gov.au through `$check-commonwealth-legislation`;
for state or territory application use its official legislation publisher. Record provision,
version, effective date, checked date, observation and evidence state. If unavailable, say
'verify against <official source>' and leave the affected proposition not verified.

## Workflow

1. Fix product/batch, role, supply chain, jurisdiction, sector, testing documents, incident
   facts and precise awareness/recall events. Escalate possible serious harm immediately to
   the qualified lawyer and safety owner while recording unknowns; do not wait for final causation.
2. Use ACCC Product Safety to discover product-specific instruments; verify each at
   legislation.gov.au or the relevant state publisher. Map ACL Part 3-3 standards, bans,
   recalls and scope/exceptions separately. Absence of a listed standard is not a safety clearance.
3. Verify ACL s 131 in current Schedule 2: qualifying awareness of death or serious injury
   or illness and possible use/foreseeable-misuse connection triggers notice within 2 days,
   subject to statutory exceptions. Capture awareness evidence, recipient, required content
   and exceptions with source evidence. Never change 'days' to 'business days' or wait for
   confirmed causation; identify-only clocks need immediate human calculation and review.
4. Check s 132 product-related-services reporting and s 128 voluntary recall notice separately;
   a recall and an injury report are not interchangeable. Verify their own events, periods,
   notices and any compulsory recall instrument before drafting an action matrix.
5. For a general safety provision record announced/proposed only; not in the ACL as at
   compilation 167 (16 September 2026). This bounded snapshot does not displace existing
   guarantees, standards, bans, recalls or reporting. Re-verify current ACL text and
   official reform publications before asserting any later duty or commencement. The
   source register includes a Treasury reform lead: verify against that publication
   before asserting ministerial priorities; its page was unavailable at this check.
6. Route concurrent guarantees to `$assess-consumer-guarantees` and incident personal-data
   issues to `$assess-australian-data-breach`. Name specialist food, therapeutic goods,
   electrical and workplace regimes for separate review rather than claiming ACL exhausts them.
7. Return urgent reporting candidates, proposed recall considerations, evidence gaps and
   responsible human decisions. No external report, recall or customer contact is authorised.

## Result contract

Lead with `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW`, `READY WITH QUALIFICATIONS`, `NOT READY`
or `OUTSIDE SCOPE`. Evidence states apply to individual propositions separately.

Product/instrument matrix; incidents and evidenced awareness; reporting triggers, candidate clocks, recipient/content and exceptions; recall/report distinction; reform status; owners and urgent reviewer decisions.
Every finding records source, version, pinpoint, checked date, assumptions, evidence
state, missing step and responsible qualified lawyer. No output is approval to act.

## Fail closed

If event time, injury definition, product scope or official source is unresolved, escalate immediately and leave the deadline provisional. Never claim no report is needed from uncertain causation, missing standards or a proposal.
