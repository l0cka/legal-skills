# Playbook usage rules

A firm may connect a drafting playbook — its standing positions on clause
points (executor powers, survivorship periods, appointment modes,
substitution patterns). The playbook is firm profile content: it guides
drafting choices, it is never authority for the law.

## Rules

1. **Adopt and note.** Where the playbook states a position that covers a
   clause choice in the precedent, adopt it and record the adoption in the
   change manifest: the clause point, the playbook position applied, and
   where in the playbook it came from.
2. **Silent means solicitor.** Where the playbook is silent on a clause
   point — or no playbook is connected — the clause choice goes to the gap
   report for the responsible solicitor. Never improvise a position.
3. **Instructions beat playbook.** Where the client's confirmed
   instructions conflict with a playbook position, the instructions
   govern and the conflict is flagged in the gap report for the solicitor
   to consider.
4. **Playbook never fills a factual field.** Names, addresses, gifts and
   shares come only from the confirmed instruction record. A playbook
   position can choose between clause alternatives; it cannot supply a
   fact.
5. **Law beats playbook.** If a playbook position conflicts with a
   verified statutory requirement, surface the conflict and stop the
   affected conclusion — the same precedence rule as all profile content
   in the source and control method.
