## Layer 1 — National Employment Standards (Part 2-2)

The Fair Work Ombudsman lists twelve entitlements (page checked 26 August
2026). Section ranges are FWO source references, qualified until read.

| # | Entitlement | Division and sections | Effect | Evidence state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Maximum weekly hours | Div 3, ss 62–64 | 38 hours plus reasonable additional hours | `VERIFIED WITH QUALIFICATIONS` |
| 2 | Requests for flexible working arrangements | Div 4, ss 65–66 | Eligible employees may request; 21-day written response; refusal only on reasonable business grounds after discussion; FWC may arbitrate | `VERIFIED` |
| 3 | Casual employment and employee choice | Div 4A, ss 66A–66K (with s 15A) | Casual may notify a choice to convert after 6 months (12 for small business); employer must respond | `VERIFIED WITH QUALIFICATIONS` |

| 7 | Community service leave | Div 8, ss 108–112 | Jury service and voluntary emergency activity | `VERIFIED` (list) |
| 8 | Long service leave | Div 9, s 113 | Preserved pre-modern-award LSL terms | `VERIFIED` (list) |
| 9 | Public holidays | Div 10, ss 114–116 | Absence on public holiday; reasonable requests to work | `VERIFIED` (list) |
| 10 | Superannuation contributions | Div 10A, ss 116A–116E | NES superannuation entitlement; amended from 1 July 2026 by the Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Act 2025 sch 1 items 73–74 — amended text not read | `VERIFIED WITH QUALIFICATIONS` |
| 11 | Notice of termination and redundancy pay | Div 11, ss 117–123 | See the termination exposure map | `VERIFIED` |

The right to disconnect is **not** an NES entitlement. It sits in Part 2-9
Div 6 (ss 333M–333Q, 333V) and is a workplace right under ss 340–341. It
applied from 26 August 2024 for non-small-business employers and from
26 August 2025 for small business employers. Evidence state: `VERIFIED`.

Instruments under s 14(5) can move a named employer out of the national
system — for example the Fair Work (State Declarations — Employer Not To Be
National System Employer) Endorsement (No. 1) 2026 (F2026L00414). Check the
Register for endorsements naming the employer. Evidence state: `VERIFIED`.
