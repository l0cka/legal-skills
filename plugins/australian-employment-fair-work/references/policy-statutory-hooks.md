# Workplace policy statutory hooks

Research snapshot checked on 26 August 2026. Each hook is labelled law or
guidance. Section numbers are routing leads until verified with
`$check-commonwealth-legislation`; non-Fair Work Acts route to the same
checker.

A policy set is reviewed against these hooks as a gap and consistency
check. The review never concludes that a policy is compliant, that a
practice is lawful, or that the positive duty has been met.
