# Employment extension source checklist

Snapshot and lawCheckedOn: 2026-10-13. Sources accessed on 2026-10-13 UTC.
These are routing leads, not cached rules. Recheck the exact applicable version,
application provisions, instruments and facts at use; a qualified lawyer must
review every conclusion. Official text controls; regulator and Treasury pages
are labelled guidance or policy. Unavailable sources block only the affected
conclusion. Earlier plugin references retain their August snapshot.

## Commonwealth employment

Official publisher: [Federal Register of Legislation — Fair Work Act 2009,
C2026C00355, compilation 73, 7 July 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/latest/downloads).
The inspected official PDFs are [Volume 1](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/2026-07-07/2026-07-07/text/original/pdf/1),
[Volume 2](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/2026-07-07/2026-07-07/text/original/pdf/2)
and [Volume 3](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/2026-07-07/2026-07-07/text/original/pdf/3).
Verify at legislation.gov.au before reliance:

| Issue | Inspected lead and limit |
| --- | --- |
| NES | Part 2-2 and s 61: minimum standards; compare actual contract clauses, not labels. Award coverage and classification require the relevant FWC instrument and duties evidence. |
| Fixed-term limits | ss 333E–333L: duration over two years, renewal and consecutive-contract conditions, exceptions, prohibited end-term effect, anti-avoidance and information statements. Read every applicable limb. Section 333G does not invalidate the entire contract. |
| Worker test | s 15AA: substance and totality of relationship, including performance; s 15AA(3) excludes specified provisions. Do not apply it universally to other statutes or earlier periods. |
| Opt-out | ss 15AB–15AD: individual election and earnings statement, notice/timing, effect and revocation; s 15C points to regulations for the contractor threshold. Income alone is not an election or a contractor-status conclusion. |
| Sham contracting | s 357: misrepresentation prohibition and employer's reasonable-belief defence; inspect belief evidence and context. |
| General protections | Part 3-1: workplace-right and discrimination tracks remain separate from discrimination statutes; s 351 includes exceptions. |
| Intentional underpayment | s 327A: covered required amounts, exclusions, conduct and payment failure; intention applies to conduct and failure. Arithmetic alone cannot establish guilt. |
| Small-business code | ss 327B–327C: assess the statutory process and Code, not just headcount; civil powers remain. |
| Civil limitation | s 544: scoped civil application period of six years from contravention. |
| Underpayment orders | s 545(5): separate six-year look-back from commencement of proceedings. |
| Criminal proceedings | s 327C(2): separate six-year period after commission of offence. |

Official [Fair Work Regulations 2009, F2026C00996, 1 October 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2009L02356/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html):
verify regulation 1.08AA for contractor threshold indexation, regulation 2.13
for the employee threshold and regulations 2.14–2.15 for fixed-term exceptions.
No dollar threshold or award rate is embedded in these new workflows.
[FWO opt-out guidance](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/independent-contractors/whole-of-relationship-test/opting-out-of-the-whole-of-relationship-test)
is a useful corroborating lead, not legislation. Retrieve the relevant income
year and calculation basis afresh. Set-offs need verified instrument wording
and official case-law support; no generic cross-period offset is assumed.

Commencement: the official [Closing Loopholes No. 2 Act 2024](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2024A00002/asmade/2024-02-26/text/5/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
s 2 table items 21 and 11, confirms s 15AA from 26 August 2024 and the sham
arrangements amendment from 27 February 2024. For s 327A, check the official
[Closing Loopholes Act 2023](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2023A00120/asmade/2023-12-14/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
s 2 item 18, and the Code declaration trigger. The 2024 Act's item 15 records
1 January 2025 for the contingent underpayment amendments; [FWO's commencement
notice](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/newsroom/news/new-criminal-underpayment-laws-have-started)
corroborates that date as guidance. Fixed-term commencement detail is a lead:
verify against legislation.gov.au, the Secure Jobs, Better Pay Act 2022 s 2
and application provisions before asserting a date. Do not rely on an old
exception regulation without checking its current status.

## Discrimination and preventive duties

Verify these inspected provisions at legislation.gov.au at the relevant date:

- [Sex Discrimination Act 1984, C2025C00597](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A02868/2025-11-01/2025-11-01/text/original/pdf),
  s 47C: reasonable and proportionate measures to eliminate specified conduct
  as far as possible; inspect conduct and person limits, not a universal duty.
- [Disability Discrimination Act 1992, inspected 1 July 2026 text](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04426/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
  s 15 employment prohibition. The retrieved index gave a version ending
  7 October 2026: current amendment/compilation status is NOT VERIFIED. Recheck
  the version at the date of conduct; do not certify this as the latest text.
- [Racial Discrimination Act 1975, C2022C00366](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00274/2022-12-13/2022-12-13/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
  s 15 employment prohibition.
- [Age Discrimination Act 2004, C2026C00266](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01302/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
  s 18 employment prohibition.
- [Australian Human Rights Commission Act 1986, C2026C00416](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03366/2026-09-18/2026-09-18/text/original/pdf),
  Part II Division 4A: s 35B inquiry on reasonable suspicion, s 35F compliance
  notices, s 35J enforcement and s 35K enforceable undertakings. Keep the
  oversight process distinct from individual complaints and court remedies.

Use AHRC guidance from humanrights.gov.au and relevant state regulator guidance
only after checking version/date; this source check does not certify any
organisation's compliance or resolve complaint limitation/election rules.

## Restraints and proposal status

[NSW Restraints of Trade Act 1976](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-1976-067),
s 4 is a routing lead. Direct HTML/PDF retrieval returned 403; search excerpts
are discovery only. **NOT VERIFIED: verify against legislation.nsw.gov.au**
before asserting the statutory effect or Supreme Court powers. Verify common-law
reasonableness, legitimate interests and severance against applicable judgments
at official court publishers; this checklist provides factors, not a case holding.

The Commonwealth non-compete ban is **announced/proposed only** at this snapshot.
[Treasury's review page](https://treasury.gov.au/review/competition-review-2023/non-compete-clauses)
sets intended 2027 timing contingent on passage of legislation. The official
[draft-legislation consultation](https://consult.treasury.gov.au/c2026-801178)
ran 7 September–2 October 2026. The [7 September ministerial release](https://ministers.treasury.gov.au/ministers/andrew-leigh-2025/media-releases/opening-door-better-and-higher-paying-jobs-banning)
describes the proposed cohort below the Fair Work employee high income threshold.
No enacted Act or legal commencement date was confirmed. Recheck Parliament
and legislation.gov.au; policy dates cannot establish a current ban.

## Reuse rather than duplicate

Use the existing employment-profile-schema.md and `configure-employment-profile`.
For services drafting use `draft-services-agreement`; for clause options use
`review-draft-against-checklist` in australian-commercial-drafting. That plugin
owns clause-bank.md and unfair-contract-terms-checklist.md, including the UTPA
2026 notes. Those notes must be reverified at their named official sources if
used; consumer-law coverage cannot be inferred for an employment contract.

For personnel data use `assess-australian-privacy-issues` and, where needed,
`route-australian-privacy-jurisdiction` and `check-australian-privacy-principles`
in australian-privacy-cybersecurity. No privacy exemption is assumed here.
For IP verify the [Copyright Act 1968](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1968A00063/2026-04-02/2026-04-02/text/original/pdf):
s 195AN(3) addresses non-assignment of authors' moral rights, subject to the
section. Other ownership/consent propositions require their own official-source
checks; the original commercial examples are not approved precedents.
