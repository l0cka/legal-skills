# Employment extension source checklist

Extension reference snapshot: 2026-10-13. Plugin-wide lawCheckedOn: 2026-08-26.
Sources accessed on 2026-10-13 UTC. These are routing leads, not cached rules.
Recheck the exact applicable version, application provisions, instruments and
facts at use; a qualified lawyer must review every conclusion. Official text
controls; regulator and Treasury pages are labelled guidance or policy.
Unavailable sources block only the affected conclusion. Earlier plugin

## Commonwealth employment

Official publisher: [Federal Register of Legislation — Fair Work Act 2009,
C2026C00355, compilation 73, 7 July
2026](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/latest/downloads). The
inspected official PDFs are [Volume
1](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/2026-07-07/2026-07-07/text/original/pdf/1),
[Volume
2](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/2026-07-07/2026-07-07/text/original/pdf/2)
and [Volume
3](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00028/2026-07-07/2026-07-07/text/original/pdf/3).
Verify at legislation.gov.au before reliance:

| Issue | Inspected lead and limit |
| --- | --- |
| NES | Part 2-2 and s 61: minimum standards; compare actual contract clauses, not labels. Award coverage and classification require the relevant FWC instrument and duties evidence. |
| Fixed-term limits | ss 333E–333L: duration over two years, renewal and consecutive-contract conditions, exceptions, prohibited end-term effect, anti-avoidance and information statements. Read every applicable limb. Section 333G does not invalidate the entire contract. |
| Worker test | s 15AA: substance and totality of relationship, including performance; s 15AA(3) excludes specified provisions. Do not apply it universally to other statutes or earlier periods. |
| Opt-out | ss 15AB–15AD: individual election and earnings statement, notice/timing, effect and revocation; s 15C points to regulations for the contractor threshold. Income alone is not an election or a contractor-status conclusion. |
| Sham contracting | s 357: misrepresentation prohibition and employer's reasonable-belief defence; inspect belief evidence and context. |
| General protections | Part 3-1: workplace-right and discrimination tracks remain separate from discrimination statutes; s 351 includes exceptions. |
| Intentional underpayment | s 327A: covered required amounts, exclusions, conduct and payment failure; intention applies to conduct and failure. Arithmetic alone cannot establish guilt. |
| Small-business code | ss 327B–327C: assess the statutory process and Code, not just headcount; civil powers remain. |
| Civil limitation | s 544: scoped civil application period of six years from contravention. |
| Underpayment orders | s 545(5): separate six-year look-back from commencement of proceedings. |
| Criminal proceedings | s 327C(2): separate six-year period after commission of offence. |

Official [Fair Work Regulations 2009, F2026C00996, 1 October
2026](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2009L02356/2026-10-01/2026-10-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html):
verify regulation 1.08AA for contractor threshold indexation, regulation 2.13
for the employee threshold and regulations 2.14–2.15 for fixed-term exceptions.
No dollar threshold or award rate is embedded in these new workflows. [FWO
opt-out
guidance](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/independent-contractors/whole-of-relationship-test/opting-out-of-the-whole-of-relationship-test)
is a useful corroborating lead, not legislation. Retrieve the relevant income
year and calculation basis afresh. Set-offs need verified instrument wording and
official case-law support; no generic cross-period offset is assumed.

Commencement: the official [Closing Loopholes No. 2 Act
2024](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2024A00002/asmade/2024-02-26/text/5/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
s 2 table items 21 and 11, confirms s 15AA from 26 August 2024 and the sham
arrangements amendment from 27 February 2024. The [policy statutory
hooks](policy-statutory-hooks.md) are the owner of the fixed-term,
SDA/AHRC-power and wage-theft commencement snapshots, including their existing
evidence states and exception-regulation leads. This extension does not re-date,
duplicate or downgrade those entries. Reverify their linked official Acts,
commencement/application tables and current regulations before reliance; their
August snapshot is not a present-law assurance.

Record-keeping methodology: verify s 557C in official Volume 3, printed pp
112–113. For proceedings concerning specified civil provisions, where an
allegation concerns a matter for which the employer failed the required
record-making/keeping, inspection or payslip obligation, the employer bears the
burden of disproving that allegation. Subsection (2) provides a
reasonable-excuse exception; subsection (3) specifies the covered civil
provisions. Missing data alone does not establish these conditions or determine
a sum or criminal guilt.

Fair Work sexual harassment: official Volume 2 identifies Part 3-5A as the
work-related sexual-harassment track, separately from Part 3-1. Verify coverage,
remedies, time limits and interaction with other claims at legislation.gov.au
before choosing a forum; this is a routing lead, not a complaint assessment.

## Discrimination and preventive duties

Verify these inspected provisions at legislation.gov.au at the relevant date:

- [Sex Discrimination Act 1984,
  C2025C00597](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A02868/2025-11-01/2025-11-01/text/original/pdf),
  s 47C: reasonable and proportionate measures to eliminate specified conduct as
  far as possible; inspect conduct and person limits, not a universal duty.
- [Disability Discrimination Act 1992, C2026C00276, compilation
  40](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04426/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
  s 15 employment prohibition. The retrieved register lists that compilation as
  ending 7 October 2026. The official [Treasury Laws Amendment (Genetic Testing
  Protections in Life Insurance and Other Measures) Act 2026,
  C2026A00035](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00035/asmade/2026-04-08/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
  s 2 item 2, confirms Schedule 1 commenced 8 October 2026. Schedule 1 items 5–6
  amend DDA s 46 for life-insurance genetic-information discrimination and set
  application rules. Read the compilation with that amendment; a later
  consolidated DDA compilation has not been confirmed. This amendment check does
  not reverify the entire DDA or assume its insurance rules govern work.
- [Racial Discrimination Act 1975,
  C2022C00366](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00274/2022-12-13/2022-12-13/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
  s 15 employment prohibition.
- [Age Discrimination Act 2004,
  C2026C00266](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01302/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html),
  s 18 employment prohibition.
- [Australian Human Rights Commission Act 1986,
  C2026C00416](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03366/2026-09-18/2026-09-18/text/original/pdf),
  Part II Division 4A: s 35B inquiry on reasonable suspicion, s 35F compliance
  notices, s 35J enforcement and s 35K enforceable undertakings. Keep the
  oversight process distinct from individual complaints and court remedies.

## Restraints and proposal status

[NSW Restraints of Trade Act
1976](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-1976-067), s
4 is a routing lead. The NSW statutory limb is unverified at this snapshot
because NSW sites block automated retrieval; search excerpts are discovery only.
**NOT VERIFIED: verify against legislation.nsw.gov.au** before asserting the
statutory effect or Supreme Court powers. Verify common-law reasonableness,
legitimate interests and severance against applicable judgments at official
court publishers; this checklist provides factors, not a case holding.

Use `track-fair-work-developments` and the [watch
register](../skills/track-fair-work-developments/references/watch-register.json),
item `non-compete-ban`, as the single owner of Commonwealth proposal status and
its official draft sources. The extension does not maintain a competing cohort,
commencement or application summary. Flag co-worker non-solicitation and
cascading clauses for that announced/proposed only track; recheck its status
before applying any later enacted law. A policy expectation is not commencement.

## Reuse rather than duplicate

Use the existing employment-profile-schema.md and
`configure-employment-profile`. For services drafting use
`draft-services-agreement`; for clause options use
`review-draft-against-checklist` in australian-commercial-drafting. That plugin
owns clause-bank.md and unfair-contract-terms-checklist.md, including the UTPA
2026 notes. Those notes must be reverified at their named official sources if
used; consumer-law coverage cannot be inferred for an employment contract.

For personnel data use `assess-australian-privacy-issues` and, where needed,
`route-australian-privacy-jurisdiction` and
`check-australian-privacy-principles` in australian-privacy-cybersecurity. No
privacy exemption is assumed here. For IP verify the [Copyright Act
1968](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1968A00063/2026-04-02/2026-04-02/text/original/pdf):
s 195AN(3) addresses non-assignment of authors' moral rights, subject to the
section. Other ownership/consent propositions require their own official-source
checks; the original commercial examples are not approved precedents.
