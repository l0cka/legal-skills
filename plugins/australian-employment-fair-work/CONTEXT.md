# Employment review vocabulary

**Employment profile**: the existing versioned factual employer map prepared by
`configure-employment-profile`. Approval is a human act; beliefs about coverage,
classification and instruments require verification, not adoption as law.

**Entitlement-source schedule**: date-specific official instrument provisions,
classifications and rates supporting each cohort and pay period. A blank rate
is a data gap, not zero liability.

**Issue matrix**: clause or conduct, applicable test, source/version/date,
evidence state, missing fact, consequence and qualified lawyer decision.

**Reform status**: distinguish in force, enacted but not yet in force, and
announced/proposed only. A target year is not a statutory commencement date.

**Exposure framework**: an auditable methodology and unresolved inputs. It is
not a final back-pay calculation, liability opinion or criminal finding.
